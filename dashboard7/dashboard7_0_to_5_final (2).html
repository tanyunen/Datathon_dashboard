Executive Analytical Brief

Analysis Method

The chart shows the full-sample median duration at each exact FX count. The table compares one and two conversions across comparable payment groups.

Observation

The median falls from 47s to 41s between zero and one conversion, then rises to 181s at two. It keeps rising through 11 conversions. Only 109 transactions recorded exactly 11 conversions, so the last point is based on a small sample.

Matched comparison

The large full-sample median increase reverses after matching, and the mean increase shrinks to 19.1 s. The 117.4 s increase in P90 shows that slower payments still differ. The 1,976,091 matched payments support a weaker overall association between one additional conversion and duration.
