Executive Analytical Brief

Analysis Method

The charts group payments by exact currency conversion count. For a like-for-like comparison, group payments with the same number of participants, transaction type and payment currency. Report the median for typical duration and P90 for slower payments.

Observation

The median falls from 47s to 41s between zero and one conversion, then rises to 181s at two. It keeps rising through 11 conversions. Only 109 transactions recorded exactly 11 conversions, so the last point is based on a small sample.

Follow-up Analysis

Also hold the directional bank-country corridor fixed to reduce the influence of differences in corridor mix. Test whether the sharp rise from one to two conversions persists for the median and P90.
