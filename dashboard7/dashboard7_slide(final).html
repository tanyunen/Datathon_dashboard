Executive Analytical Brief

Analysis Method

The headline charts group by exact conversion count and pool over route size, transaction type and currency. These are potential confounders; compare within participant count, type and currency before attributing a duration difference. Median shows typical duration; P90 shows the slower tail.

Question and observation

Question: Does duration change with observed FX conversion count? Median duration is 47s at zero, 41s at one, then 181s at two. It rises through 11 conversions, but only 109 transactions recorded exactly 11 conversions, so the tail estimate is uncertain.

Reconstructed rule vs baseline

Among records with exactly one observed conversion (baseline, n=5,895,841), median duration is 41 seconds. For exactly two conversions (n=2,017,992), it is 181 seconds—140 seconds longer, or 4.4× the baseline. This is a raw association; the comparison should be repeated within the same corridor, route size and transaction type.

Follow-up Analysis

Also hold the directional bank-country corridor fixed to reduce the influence of differences in corridor mix. Test whether the sharp rise from one to two conversions persists for the median and P90.
