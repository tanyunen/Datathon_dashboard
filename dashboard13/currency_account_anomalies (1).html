Analysis

INR and CNY. Romance scams account for 429 of 450 INR labels (95.33%) and 426 of 567 CNY labels (75.13%). Consumers sent all 855 payments. Consumer accounts received 328 of the INR cases and 312 of the CNY cases. Business accounts received the remaining 101 and 114, respectively.

USD. Fat-finger mistakes account for 316 of 952 labels (33.19%). They appear in all four account pairs. Business-to-consumer payments have the most cases (179), while consumer-to-consumer payments have the highest observed rate (1.96 per 10,000, compared with 1.87 for business-to-consumer).

Reconstructed rule

Across the six currencies, all 1,238 romance-scam labels had consumer senders and all 548 CEO-scam labels had business senders. Both groups included business and consumer recipients. For CEO scams, business-to-consumer payments had 395 cases versus 153 for business-to-business payments, with similar rates of 1.13 and 1.06 per 10,000.

Currency mix. For romance scams, the consumer-to-consumer and consumer-to-business rates fell from 10.26 and 8.73 to 3.25 and 2.99 after excluding INR and CNY. USD alone reversed the order (4.88 and 6.34).

Ring share = cases of a label ÷ all labelled payments in that currency. Matrix rate = cases in a cell ÷ all payments for that account pair × 10,000. Source: supplied fictitious payment dataset.
