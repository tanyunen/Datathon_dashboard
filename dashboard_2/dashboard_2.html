Finding, interpretation, and limits

1. Tested question and rule vs baseline

Question: Do records marked N have a different mean duration from Y? Yes: for all valid-duration records, N is 111.74 seconds (14.65%) higher than Y, the baseline. This is an unadjusted association; it does not establish that an incomplete path caused longer duration.

Continuous paths (Y) account for 92.33% (9,232,796 records) of the dataset with a mean duration of 762.94 seconds (~763s), establishing the operational speed baseline.

2. Participant-count pattern

Incomplete-path share tends to rise in groups with more route participants. The high-count groups have small samples, so their rates are less stable.

3. Field meaning

CONTINUITY_QFLAG = N means the recorded route is incomplete or discontinuous. The literal ... marker appears in exactly 767,204 N paths and none of the 9,232,796 Y paths; all Y path segment counts match participant counts. This synthetic-format rule does not mean payment failure, settlement delay, or liquidity lockup.

4. Business follow-up

Compare records within the same participant count and transaction type, and include sample sizes, before interpreting the duration difference.
