Analysis Brief

Zero-FX payments. EUR and USD recorded zero FX steps in 19.85% and 16.49% of payments, respectively, versus 0.19%–4.23% for the three lowest-ranked eligible currencies.

Multiple FX steps. Among the 21 currencies with at least 50,000 payments, higher usage was associated with a lower share of payments with two or more FX steps (Spearman ρ = −0.55, p ≈ 0.0097). Across all 155 currencies, the association was near zero (ρ = +0.055, p ≈ 0.500). P values are approximate and two-sided. Both analyses pool all corridors.
