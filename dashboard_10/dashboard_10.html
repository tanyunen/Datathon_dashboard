Analysis & reconstructed rule

Currency mix. Romance scams make up 95.33% of INR and 75.13% of CNY labels. Consumers sent all 855 of these cases. Fat-finger mistakes lead in USD and EUR, at 33.19% and 39.33%.

Sender rule. All 1,195 romance-scam cases had consumer senders. All 472 CEO-scam cases had business senders. Account replacement appears in all four pairs.

Recipient pattern. For CEO scams, B → C had 338 cases versus 134 for B → B, at 1.14 and 1.09 per 10,000. For romance scams, C → C and C → B rates were 11.59 and 10.01. In USD and EUR alone, these rates were 3.36 and 3.28.
