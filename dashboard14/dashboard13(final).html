Rule, method and analysis

Rule

Count a payment once per distinct BIC8 found between the first and last banks in its path. Rank intermediaries by payment count. Divide each count by all eligible payments to obtain its share.

Method

Keep complete paths whose BIC8 node count matches the recorded participant count. For timing, group by origin bank, destination bank, currency, payment type, participant count and FX conversion count. Compare the two most-used paths per group, with at least 100 payments per path. Two groups qualified.

Analysis

On the EUR corridor, the path via 0309CHZ0 had a 94 s lower median and a 96.5 s lower P90. On the MYR corridor, both paths had a 33 s median. Each timing result describes the full path for its specific bank pair and payment profile.

Operational use

If both EUR paths are available on comparable terms, pilot the route via 0309CHZ0 and monitor median and P90.
