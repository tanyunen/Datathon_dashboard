Finding, interpretation, and limits

1. Tested question and result

Direct records are the largest group at 63.23%; Indirect is 32.61%, and Covered is 4.16%. These are dataset shares, not SWIFT market shares.

Question: Is the incomplete-path share the same across transaction types? It is not in this dataset, though the comparison does not establish why.

2. Reconstructed rule vs baseline

Among records of each type, N occurs in 4.99% of Direct (baseline), 12.29% of Indirect, and 12.32% of Covered. That is about 2.46× and 2.47× the Direct rate, respectively. It is an association; it does not explain why a path is incomplete.

1. Transaction types involving multi-party routing exhibit significantly higher path discontinuity.

2. 2-participant routes occur exclusively with Direct transactions.

3. Exact structural checks

Across all 10 million rows, 2 participants occurs only with Direct; every route with 3 or more participants is Indirect or Covered. The literal ... marker appears in exactly 767,204 paths, all flagged N; it appears in none of the 9,232,796 Y paths. For Y rows, the semicolon-delimited path segment count matches PARTICIPANT_COUNT.

4. Field meaning and limit

CONTINUITY_QFLAG = N means the recorded path is incomplete or discontinuous. These are exact patterns in this synthetic file, not proof of payment failure, rejection, or a compliance hold.
