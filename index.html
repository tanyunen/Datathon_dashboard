Key findings from this dataset

Business sender amounts

Business senders are 78.75% of records. Their median normalized amount is $5,537.36 versus $772.62 for Consumer senders; this is descriptive, not a payment-purpose explanation.

Largest party-type pair

Business-to-Consumer records are 56.04% of the dataset (5,603,692 records). Party-type fields do not establish whether these are payroll or other payouts.

Path continuity by transaction type

The incomplete-path flag is N for 4.99% of Direct, 12.29% of Indirect, and 12.32% of Covered records. N is not a payment failure.

Route participant count

Mean duration rises from 663 seconds at two participants to 841 at three and 1,042 at four. This is an observed association; large-count groups are sparse.

Time pattern

In Malaysia time (UTC+8), 23:00 is the busiest hour and Friday the busiest day. These describe the synthetic timestamp distribution.

Anomaly labels

7,006 records (0.0701%) carry one of the analyzed anomaly labels. Normalized payment amounts are not confirmed losses or fraud outcomes.
