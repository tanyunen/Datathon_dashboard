Question and reconstructed rule

Question: Does the rate of records with a listed ANOMALY_TYPE label vary by local hour within each TRANSACTION_TYPE_CODE?

Rule: Convert EPOCH_SECONDS to Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. For every type and hour, calculate labelled records / all valid-timestamp records x 10,000; compare with that type's pooled all-hour rate.

What the data shows

The highest observed type/hour cell is Indirect at 13:00: 108 of 101,213 records (10.671 per 10,000, 1.41x its type baseline). This is an exploratory maximum across 72 cells, not proof of a recurring peak.

Interpretation: Hour/type differences can identify where to inspect label composition next. They do not establish operator fatigue, settlement stress, or that automation would prevent labelled records.

Method and limits: All 10,000,000 records had valid timestamps. Hours use Asia/Kuala_Lumpur; no sender time-zone is provided, and BIC country alone cannot resolve local time in multi-zone countries. These are hourly label rates, not clearing windows. A listed label means a non-empty dataset annotation; an unlabelled record is not confirmed normal. Rates are unadjusted for currency, route, or amount. The highest cell is descriptive and should be checked in another sample before drawing operational conclusions.

Source: supplied fictitious payment dataset. Times are Asia/Kuala_Lumpur. Generated by generate_dashboards9_10.py. Rates are descriptive and apply only to this dataset.
