      Executive Analytical Brief

      Currency mix changes with payment direction

      EUR accounted for 52.8% of Europe-to-Americas payments and 9.1% in the reverse direction, a gap of 43.7 percentage points. USD accounted for 63.4% of Americas-to-Europe payments.

      The pattern also appears when the destination is held fixed

      Among payments to Europe, EUR accounted for 56.1% of payments from Europe, 9.8% from Asia and 9.1% from the Americas. In this dataset, currency choice is associated with the ordering bank’s region even when the destination region is the same.

      Hypothesis to test

      The ordering bank’s location may help explain currency choice across several country corridors. Compare payments to the same destination country from several origin countries. A repeated pattern would support the hypothesis. If the gap is concentrated in one or two country corridors, the conclusion should focus on those corridors.
